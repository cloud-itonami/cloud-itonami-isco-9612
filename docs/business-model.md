# Business Model: Refuse Sorting Facility Scheduling Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-9612`
- ISCO-08: `9612`
- Occupation: Refuse Sorters
- Social impact: worker-safety, public-health, recycling-diversion

## Customer

- recycling / materials-recovery / waste-sorting facilities
- independent refuse-sorting-facility crews / sorting-labour cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- sorting-log/throughput/progress-record logging
- protective-equipment/consumables procurement coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to facility safety supervisors

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a sorting-operation-execution decision, ever
- no direct finalization of a facility-safety-clearance decision (e.g.
  declaring a facility cleared for safety), ever
- no override of a facility safety supervisor's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (hazardous-material-handling hazard,
  sorting-line-machinery hazard, equipment-condition hazard) always
  route to human sign-off, regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
